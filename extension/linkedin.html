Search LinkedIn on every Find jobs?

The Jobadabra app can read your LinkedIn job searches every time you press Find jobs, in the background with no window, using your LinkedIn sign-in from this Chrome.

  • Chrome will ask for two permissions, once: LinkedIn pages and cookies.
  • Only two LinkedIn cookies are read (li_at, JSESSIONID) and sent only to the Jobadabra app on this Mac (127.0.0.1). The app keeps them in your Keychain.
  • Turn it off in Jobadabra → Settings → Connections → LinkedIn: the sign-in is deleted and Chrome's permissions are given back.

LinkedIn may limit accounts it thinks are automated; we read slowly and only your job searches.
